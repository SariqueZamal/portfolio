Core Business & Partnership FAQs

10 Key Questions
Is Digital Cron an agency or a software company?
Digital Cron is a technology partner that provides custom web development and practical automation systems. We do not sell generic off-the-shelf software subscriptions; we engineer bespoke websites, AI reception workflows, and booking integrations specifically scoped around how your business operates.
Do you work with existing websites?
Yes. You don't necessarily need to rebuild your website from scratch. We can evaluate your current website and integrate AI reception, automated booking calendars, or CRM lead capture pipelines into your existing platform where practical.
Do you build custom websites?
Yes. Every new website we deliver is custom-built with clean, semantic code optimized for fast page load speeds, pristine Core Web Vitals, and conversion clarity. We do not use bloated page builder themes that slow down your site.
Can you connect my existing CRM?
Yes. We integrate with major CRM and scheduling platforms via secure webhooks and APIs. Lead details, booking parameters, and qualification notes are synchronized directly into your team's existing workflow.
Can you automate phone calls?
Yes. We can deploy automated AI voice receptionists configured to answer incoming calls 24/7, answer common business inquiries, collect customer contact details, and schedule appointment slots directly into your calendar.
Can you automate appointment booking?
Yes. We configure frictionless calendar booking workflows synchronized with Google Calendar, Cal.com, or your practice management software. The system respects your team's real-time availability and buffer times.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes. Our systems are built primarily for independent practices, local service contractors, real estate teams, boutique stays, and growing service businesses that handle customer enquiries and appointments daily.
How much does a project cost?
Because every system is scoped around the business and its operational workflow, investment varies based on whether you need a high-performance website, specific automation modules, or an end-to-end business system. The first step is a free consultation to map your requirements and provide a clear, tailored proposal.
How long does a project take?
Delivery timelines depend on scope. A targeted conversion website or single automation workflow typically takes 2 to 3 weeks, while a comprehensive business system with AI voice reception and CRM integration typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.
Do you guarantee more leads or revenue?
No responsible technology provider can guarantee a specific revenue outcome. Digital Cron focuses on building and optimizing the systems that improve the customer journey, eliminate response delays, and make lead handling more efficient.
Do I need to rebuild my website?
No. If your current website already provides a solid technical and visual foundation, we can focus entirely on conversion improvements, calendar integrations, AI reception, or CRM automation without rebuilding your entire site. Rebuilds are only recommended when legacy code or slow page builders create insurmountable bottlenecks.
Can the system include human staff?
Yes. Our systems are built to assist human teams, not replace them. When a customer inquiry requires clinical judgment, complex quotation, or urgent human attention, the system immediately routes caller notes and details to your team for direct handling.
What happens after I contact Digital Cron?
We follow a transparent 5-step process: (1) You tell us about your business and how you handle inquiries. (2) We map the operational opportunity and review friction points. (3) You receive a clear, tailored system proposal with fixed milestones. (4) We build, test, and integrate your website and automation workflows. (5) We launch and verify the complete customer journey.

Website Development & Technical SEO

High Performance
Can you redesign our existing website without hurting current search rankings?
Yes. Website redesigns are handled with strict technical SEO safeguards. We audit your existing ranking URLs, configure 301 redirect mappings, and preserve title and heading hierarchies while upgrading page load speed, mobile UX, and conversion paths.
What makes an "SEO-focused website" different from a standard website?
Standard websites are often built on bloated visual builders that slow down search crawlers. An SEO-focused website is engineered from clean semantic code with optimized heading hierarchies, structured Schema.org JSON-LD data, fast load speed Core Web Vitals, and local search service area architecture built directly into the site structure.
How do you achieve fast page load speeds?
We eliminate heavy third-party plugins and monolithic CMS themes. Every page is hand-coded with lightweight, optimized stylesheets, asynchronous scripts, modern WebP image compression, preconnect server hints, and global edge caching to ensure instant rendering on any mobile network.
Will our website look and function perfectly across all mobile devices?
Yes. Because over 65% of local service and healthcare searches originate on mobile phones, we build mobile-first. Booking forms, phone call triggers, navigation menus, and media galleries are engineered for single-thumb ergonomics and responsive display across iOS, Android, and tablets.

AI Voice Reception & Communication Automation

24/7 Operations
What happens if a phone caller asks a question the AI cannot answer?
The AI is engineered with strict guardrails and human escalation protocols. If a caller asks a question outside its approved knowledge boundaries, the system gracefully summarizes the caller's details, transfers the call directly to an on-duty staff member, or dispatches an instant SMS/email alert for immediate follow-up.
How does AI Email Automation work with our existing business inbox?
We connect an intelligent parsing layer to your inbound email inbox or contact form submissions. The system categorizes incoming leads by intent (e.g. quote request, reschedule, general inquiry), drafts contextual, approved email responses, and triggers multi-stage follow-up sequences so inquiries receive prompt attention.
What is the difference between a simple chatbot and a trained AI assistant?
Generic chatbots rely on rigid decision trees that break when a visitor phrases a question unusually. Our trained AI assistants understand context, handle nuanced inquiries, qualify visitor intent through interactive questions, and securely trigger calendar booking or CRM webhook dispatches without manual staff intervention.

Booking Systems & Lead Management

Connected Core
How do your Website + Booking systems prevent double-bookings?
Our booking engines synchronize two-way in real time with your team's live calendars (Google Calendar, Outlook, or specialized practice software). As soon as an appointment slot is taken—whether booked online, scheduled over the phone, or blocked out manually—it immediately disappears from the customer-facing booking interface.
Can the system collect payment deposits or card authorizations before booking?
Yes. For businesses with high cancellation or no-show risk (such as spas, salons, and cosmetic practices), we integrate secure card deposit safeguards. The slot is only confirmed once the client enters their card details or pays a nominal deposit, protecting practitioner time.
How does automated lead follow-up work for home service contractors?
When a homeowner submits a quote request, the system instantly validates their zip code, captures project details, and fires an instant SMS confirmation to the customer while simultaneously dispatching an alert to the on-duty field technician. Follow-up SMS check-ins are sent automatically if the lead has not booked within 2 hours.
