Project Scoping & Commercials

How much does a project cost?
Every project is scoped individually based on your specific requirements, complexity, third-party integrations, and implementation needs. We do not use rigid public packages because every business has a different technology stack and operational goal. After an initial discovery consultation, we provide a transparent, tailored proposal.
Do you work with international clients?
Yes. Digital Cron works with clients across the United States, Europe, Australia, the Middle East, and India. All communications, documentation, and agreements are conducted in clear, international English.
Do you offer ongoing maintenance and support?
Yes. Every project includes a post-launch support window. Additionally, we offer ongoing optimization retainers covering security updates, continuous prompt refinement, speed monitoring, and workflow additions.
Web Development

Websites & Landing Pages

How long does a website project take from start to finish?
Standard business website and landing page builds typically take between 3 to 6 weeks. More complex applications or multi-language sites take between 6 to 8 weeks, including thorough mobile and cross-browser testing.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We regularly rebuild existing websites to dramatically improve load speed, modernize visual branding, enhance mobile responsiveness, and connect automated lead-capture pipelines.
Will my website be mobile-friendly and fast?
Absolutely. We build with a mobile-first philosophy, optimizing media assets, minimizing JavaScript execution, and applying modern CSS techniques to guarantee sub-second load times on mobile networks.
Do you provide SEO foundations?
Yes. Every website we build includes complete technical SEO: semantic HTML5 structure, structured JSON-LD data (Organization, Service, FAQ, Breadcrumbs), Open Graph social previews, XML sitemaps, and optimized meta tags.
AI & Workflows

AI Automation & CRM Integration

What software tools and CRMs can you integrate?
We connect to virtually any platform with an API or webhook support, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho, ActiveCampaign, Google Workspace, Cal.com, Calendly, Stripe, Twilio, Slack, WhatsApp Business, and custom SQL databases.
How do you prevent AI chatbots from making false claims?
We implement strict Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) guardrails. The AI is restricted to referencing your verified company documentation. If an inquiry falls outside its approved knowledge, it gracefully defers and routes the question to a human team member.
Can an AI voice agent book appointments on our live calendar?
Yes. Our voice agents connect directly to live calendar APIs (such as Google Calendar, Cal.com, or CRM schedules) to check real-time availability, confirm slots with the caller, and dispatch confirmation reminders via SMS or WhatsApp.
