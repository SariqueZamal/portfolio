OPERATIONAL FRICTION

The Gap Between Website Traffic and Booked Customers

A website can attract visitors without turning those visitors into conversations, appointments or bookings. Leads may be missed when enquiries arrive after hours, staff are busy, forms are not monitored or scheduling requires repeated back-and-forth.

Typical Manual Customer Journey

Where Enquiries Get Delayed

How enquiries typically move through disconnected communication channels before staff can intervene:

  • × Visitor lands on site: Generic contact form or phone number with no immediate qualification.
  • × Delayed response: Enquiries sit in unmonitored inboxes or voicemails after business hours.
  • × Manual questions: Staff plays phone tag asking basic qualifying questions.
  • × Scheduling back-and-forth: Multiple messages required to agree on an available calendar slot.
  • × Manual follow-up: No automated confirmations or reminders, leading to missed opportunities.
The result can be slower response times, unnecessary administrative work and opportunities that require more effort to convert.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear Answers for Business Owners

Honest, transparent details about our implementation approach, scope, and technical deliverables.

Is Digital Cron an agency or a software company?
Digital Cron is a technology partner that provides custom web development and practical automation systems. We do not sell generic off-the-shelf software subscriptions; we engineer bespoke websites, AI reception workflows, and booking integrations specifically scoped around how your business operates.
Do you work with existing websites?
Yes. You don't necessarily need to rebuild your website from scratch. We can evaluate your current website and integrate AI reception, automated booking calendars, or CRM lead capture pipelines into your existing platform where practical.
Do you build custom websites?
Yes. Every new website we deliver is custom-built with clean, semantic code optimized for fast page load speeds, pristine Core Web Vitals, and conversion clarity. We do not use bloated page builder themes that slow down your site.
Can you connect my existing CRM?
Yes. We integrate with major CRM and scheduling platforms via secure webhooks and APIs. Lead details, booking parameters, and qualification notes are synchronized directly into your team's existing workflow.
Can you automate phone calls?
Yes. We can deploy automated AI voice receptionists configured to answer incoming calls 24/7, answer common business inquiries, collect customer contact details, and schedule appointment slots directly into your calendar.
Can you automate appointment booking?
Yes. We configure frictionless calendar booking workflows synchronized with Google Calendar, Cal.com, or your practice management software. The system respects your team's real-time availability and buffer times.
Do you work with small businesses?
Yes. Our systems are built primarily for independent practices, local service contractors, real estate teams, boutique stays, and growing service businesses that handle customer enquiries and appointments daily.
How much does a project cost?
Because every system is scoped around the business and its operational workflow, investment varies based on whether you need a high-performance website, specific automation modules, or an end-to-end business system. The first step is a free consultation to map your requirements and provide a clear, tailored proposal.
How long does a project take?
Delivery timelines depend on scope. A targeted conversion website or single automation workflow typically takes 2 to 3 weeks, while a comprehensive business system with AI voice reception and CRM integration typically takes 4 to 6 weeks.
Do you guarantee more leads or revenue?
No responsible technology provider can guarantee a specific revenue outcome. Digital Cron focuses on building and optimizing the systems that improve the customer journey, eliminate response delays, and make lead handling more efficient.
