How It Works

The 5-Step Delivery Process

A disciplined, transparent approach to understanding your business and building a digital foundation that works.

01

Tell Us About Your Business

Share your website, industry, and how customers currently contact you. We identify where you lose time and where leads drop off.

02

We Map the Opportunity

We review your website and current workflow to identify specific areas where a faster website, automated qualification, or self-service booking could help.

03

We Recommend a System

You receive a practical, transparent proposal tailored to your actual requirements with clear deliverables, timeline, and scope.

04

We Build & Integrate

Once approved, Digital Cron designs, codes, and connects the complete system—integrating your website, calendar, and CRM tools.

05

Launch & Improve

We test every workflow rigorously across mobile and desktop, deploy to your domain, provide a full walkthrough, and make improvements after launch.
