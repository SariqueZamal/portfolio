Targeted Business Systems

Business Solutions Engineered for Clarity

Explore practical architectures designed around specific operational bottlenecks. We map the real problem to a robust workflow, delivering tangible business results without bloated software.

System 01

Lead Capture & Instant Qualification

Turn passive website visitors into scored, high-intent sales opportunities within seconds.

1. The Problem

Visitors submit a generic form and wait 24–48 hours for a response. By then, over 50% have contacted competitors or lost interest.

2. The Workflow

Website Form / Chatbot → Instant AI Intent & Budget Scoring → Real-Time CRM Contact Creation → Priority Slack Alert to Sales Rep.

3. The Business Benefit

Sub-minute response times, zero lead slippage, and immediate calendar bookings with qualified decision-makers.

System 02

Appointment & Calendar Booking Systems

Eliminate back-and-forth emails and missed calls for consultations and clinic appointments.

1. The Problem

Staff spend hours each day coordinating calendars, answering rescheduling inquiries, and sending manual reminders.

2. The Workflow

Client selects slot on custom booking UI / AI voice agent → Auto-sync to Google Calendar/Cal.com → Instant WhatsApp/Email confirmation → Automated 24h & 2h reminders.

3. The Business Benefit

Significantly reduced no-show rates, 24/7 self-service scheduling, and full staff schedule visibility.

System 03

24/7 AI Receptionist & Phone Triage

Ensure every phone inquiry is professionally handled, even when your office is closed.

1. The Problem

After-hours and peak-time callers reach voicemail, resulting in lost revenue for service and clinical businesses.

2. The Workflow

Inbound Call → Low-Latency AI Voice Agent answers with natural voice → Understands inquiry context → Checks calendar / books appointment → Dispatches call summary to CRM.

3. The Business Benefit

100% call capture rate, reduced front-desk phone load, and continuous lead intake around the clock.

System 04

Client Onboarding & Document Collection

Automate welcoming agreements, intake forms, and folder creation when a new client signs.

1. The Problem

Onboarding delays frustrate new clients and consume valuable project management time creating folders and sending manual links.

2. The Workflow

Contract Signed / Deposit Paid → Dedicated Google Drive / Notion workspace generated → Automated welcome sequence with tailored intake checklist → Account manager alerted.

3. The Business Benefit

Fast, professional client first-impressions and complete operational consistency across every account.

System 05

Internal Operations & Data Reporting

Automate cross-platform data synchronization and weekly leadership summaries.

1. The Problem

Managers manually export CSV files from three different platforms to create weekly performance reports.

2. The Workflow

Scheduled n8n webhook → Queries CRM, Stripe, and Analytics APIs → Normalizes metrics into central database → Delivers formatted executive digest to Slack/Email.

3. The Business Benefit

Real-time visibility into operational metrics without hours of manual data collation.
