SYSTEM BLUEPRINTS

Five Core Industry Solutions

Each blueprint demonstrates how websites, lead capture, qualification, and automated scheduling connect to eliminate operational friction.

SOLUTION 01 • CLINICS & HEALTH

Clinic Patient Enquiry & Booking System

Designed for independent practices, dental clinics, and specialized medical centers.

The Problem

Staff spend valuable hours answering repetitive administrative questions and coordinating appointment requests. Inquiries arriving after hours go unanswered or require repeated phone tag.

Example Workflow

Website → Enquiry → Assistant → Information Collection → Appointment Request → Confirmation → Reminder → Staff/CRM. Includes emergency escalation routing.

Designed Outcome

Frictionless 24/7 patient intake, reduced front-desk administrative load, and structured appointment requests with automated SMS confirmations.

SOLUTION 02 • HOSPITALITY

Hospitality Direct Booking & Guest Enquiry System

Designed for boutique stays, independent hotels, and hospitality venues.

The Problem

Prospective guests ask repetitive questions about room amenities, check-in policies, and availability. Direct booking inquiries get delayed, causing visitors to return to third-party platforms.

Example Workflow

Website → Guest Questions → Availability Check → Booking Request → Confirmation → Reminder/Follow-Up. Streamlined customer communication.

Designed Outcome

Faster answers to routine guest questions, encouragement of direct website bookings, and automated confirmation messages that reduce avoidable missed bookings.

Explore Hospitality Blueprint →
SOLUTION 03 • REAL ESTATE

Real Estate Lead Qualification System

Designed for real estate agencies, independent brokers, and property teams.

The Problem

Agents waste valuable hours manually qualifying unverified property inquiries, answering basic questions about listings, and coordinating showing times across email and text.

Example Workflow

Property Page → Enquiry → Buyer/Renter Questions → Budget/Timeline → Qualification → Agent Notification → Viewing Request → Follow-Up.

Designed Outcome

Verified buyer and renter intent details collected upfront, automatic inquiry routing to listing agents, and self-service calendar viewing coordination.

Explore Real Estate Blueprint →
SOLUTION 04 • SOLAR

Solar Lead Qualification System

Designed for residential solar installers and clean energy contractors.

The Problem

Solar sales teams spend considerable time calling unqualified leads only to find out they are renters, have heavily shaded roofs, or live outside the service area.

Example Workflow

Landing Page → Property Details → Homeowner Status → Energy Information → Qualification → Consultation Request → Sales Follow-Up.

Designed Outcome

Screened inquiries with confirmed homeowner status, electric bill range, and roof type before consultation booking, improving sales team efficiency.

Explore Solar Blueprint →
SOLUTION 05 • HOME SERVICES

Home Service Lead-to-Job Automation

Designed for HVAC, electrical, plumbing, roofing, and contractor teams.

The Problem

Technicians and dispatchers miss high-urgency calls while working in the field. Customers waiting for callback quotes quickly call the next local contractor on Google.

Example Workflow

Website → Service Request → Location → Job Details → Photos → Qualification → Booking → CRM → Team Notification.

Designed Outcome

Structured job intake capturing location, urgency, and photos, automated customer calendar booking for site estimates, and instant team dispatch alerts.
