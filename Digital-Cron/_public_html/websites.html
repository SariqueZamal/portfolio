Common Questions

Web Development FAQ

How long does a website take to build?
Most business websites and landing pages are completed within 3 to 6 weeks. The timeline depends on the number of pages, custom features like booking integration, and how quickly content and feedback are provided.
Can you redesign my existing website?
Yes. We regularly rebuild outdated websites to improve speed, modern aesthetics, mobile responsiveness, and booking flows while preserving existing search engine URLs.
Can you integrate appointment booking tools?
Yes. We can integrate self-service booking systems like Cal.com, Calendly, or custom booking forms directly into your website so customers can choose appointments seamlessly.
How much does a website project cost?
Every project is scoped individually based on your requirements, technical complexity, and existing technology stack. After a discovery consultation, we provide a transparent, tailored proposal.
