7 Business Processes You Can Automate This Month.

By Sarique Zamal • September 2026

Running a business shouldn't mean spending your entire day answering calls, replying to emails, scheduling appointments, and chasing potential customers. Automation can free you from repetitive admin and let you focus on growth.

Here are seven high‑impact processes you can start automating this month.

  1. Customer Calls and Missed Phone Inquiries – Implement a call‑routing system with voicemail‑to‑email transcription and instant notification to your team. A missed call triggers an automated email/SMS asking the caller to schedule a call.
  2. Website Enquiry Follow‑Up – Connect your contact form to a CRM workflow that sends a personalized thank‑you email instantly and adds the lead to a nurturing sequence.
  3. Appointment Booking – Replace back‑and‑forth email scheduling with a Calendly/Cal.com booking page that syncs directly to Google Calendar and sends reminder texts.
  4. Email Management – Use AI‑powered email triage to sort incoming messages, flag urgent enquiries, and draft quick replies for common queries.
  5. Client Onboarding – Automate the delivery of welcome packets, intake forms, and contract signing via a single onboarding portal.
  6. Feedback & Review Requests – After a job is completed, trigger an automated SMS/email asking for a review and optionally a short Net Promoter Score survey.
  7. Weekly Performance Digests – Pull data from your CRM, calendar, and email stats into a weekly PDF or Slack message summarising leads, booked meetings, and revenue indicators.
