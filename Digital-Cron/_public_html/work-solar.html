Who This Is For

This system blueprint is designed for regional solar contractors, installation teams, and renewable energy providers who spend money on local marketing or search traffic and need a dependable way to filter unqualified inquiries before their sales consultants get on the phone.

Common Operational Problems

  • Unqualified Lead Fatigue: Sales representatives spending hours calling renters or homes with heavily shaded roofs who cannot install solar.
  • Slow Speed to Lead: Homeowners requesting quotes on evenings or weekends waiting 24 to 48 hours for a reply, by which time they have contacted competitors.
  • Manual Data Collection: Sales staff spending the first 15 minutes of every call manually collecting monthly electric bills and property specs.
  • Consultation Drop-Off: Unscheduled phone inquiries falling out of the funnel due to endless phone tag.

The Proposed Digital Cron System

We deploy a streamlined, conversion-optimized landing interface paired with a multi-step qualification intake funnel. Instead of a generic contact form, the homeowner answers five essential qualification questions (home ownership status, estimated monthly electric bill, roof condition, and shading). High-intent qualifying prospects are immediately offered an interactive calendar slot for an on-site or virtual solar assessment, while your CRM receives complete lead data in real time.

End-to-End Workflow Pipeline

The system routes homeowner inquiries through a disciplined 7-stage automated pipeline:

STAGE 01

Landing Page & Intent Capture

Front-End Core

The homeowner lands on a fast, lightweight mobile interface highlighting local utility rates, solar incentives, and a straightforward eligibility assessment.

STAGE 02

Homeowner & Property Verification

Qualification Filter

The visitor confirms property ownership (Single Family Homeowner vs. Renter) and roof age to immediately verify technical feasibility.

STAGE 03

Energy Consumption Assessment

Triage Engine

The homeowner selects their average monthly electricity spend range ($150–$300, $300–$500, $500+) and utility provider to estimate system capacity needs.

STAGE 04

Automated Lead Scoring

Edge Logic

The system scores the enquiry based on roof suitability, ownership, and bill amount. High-scoring leads are routed directly to priority scheduling.

STAGE 05

Instant Consultation Booking

Calendar Sync

Qualified homeowners select a live slot on your solar design consultant's calendar for a 20-minute proposal review or site visit.

STAGE 06

CRM Sync & Sales Dispatch

Webhook Pipeline

Lead parameters, electric bill data, and booking confirmation are instantly written into your CRM or Google Sheets, alerting sales leadership via email/SMS.

STAGE 07

Automated Confirmation & Reminders

SMS / Email

The homeowner receives an immediate calendar invite and SMS confirmation with guidelines on what bills to prepare for the consultation.
