❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💡 How does PDF to Word conversion work locally?

The tool uses PDF.js to parse object tokens and font tables directly within the browser, extracting clean text sequences and wrapping them into standard Microsoft Word compatible MIME document packages.

💡 Can I edit the converted file in Microsoft Word or Google Docs?

Yes. The generated document opens seamlessly in Microsoft Word, Google Docs, Apple Pages, and LibreOffice Writer with full editing capabilities.

💡 What happens if my PDF is a scanned image?

If your PDF contains scanned bitmap images rather than digital text, please use our Scanned PDF to Text tool which utilizes local optical character recognition.

💡 Are tables and columns retained?

Tabular data is structured into sequential paragraphs and tab-delimited text blocks, allowing easy reformatting inside your word processor.

💡 Is there a page limit?

There is no page limit. High-volume documents will simply take a few moments longer depending on your device's processor.
