ℹ️ About & Educational Context: Add Watermark to PDF Online - Custom Text Watermark

Digital Cron Tools PDF Watermark engine stamps translucent vector text watermarks diagonally across every page of your PDF document. Implemented via pdf-lib, watermark typography is calculated relative to page bounding boxes (MediaBox) and layered with standardized alpha opacity transparency. Ideal for safeguarding intellectual drafts, legal discovery documents, and proprietary whitepapers.

Unlike traditional online converter services that upload confidential files to centralized cloud servers, Digital Cron Tools runs 100% inside your browser memory sandbox. Your sensitive contracts, financial statements, medical records, and business correspondence never traverse the network, satisfying stringent GDPR, HIPAA, and corporate data governance policies.

By eliminating server round-trips and queuing latencies, processing speed is constrained solely by your local device hardware. Enjoy unrestricted access, unlimited daily usage, and complete privacy assurance on every operation.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

💡 Can I customize the watermark text and opacity?

Yes, you can customize the watermark text (e.g. CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, SAMPLE), adjust the font size, and fine-tune opacity.

💡 Does the watermark apply to all pages?

Yes, the watermark is applied consistently across every single page of your document.

💡 Will the watermark block underlying text?

By setting a low opacity (e.g. 0.2 to 0.4), underlying document text remains legible while the watermark remains clearly visible.

💡 Are my documents uploaded to a cloud server?

No. The watermarking routine runs 100% inside your local web browser.

💡 Does it work on large multi-page reports?

Yes, the tool iterates through pages in milliseconds using local WebAssembly stream processing.
