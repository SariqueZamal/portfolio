ℹ️ About & Educational Context: CTR (Click-Through Rate) Calculator

E-commerce profitability hinges on rigorous unit economics, analyzing the mathematical interplay between Gross Margin, Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Return on Ad Spend (ROAS), and Customer Lifetime Value (LTV). The CTR (Click-Through Rate) Calculator models transaction fee structures, variable fulfillment overhead, and platform marketplace commissions to determine precise breakeven and target profit thresholds.

Many retail operators conflate 'markup' (profit expressed as a percentage of cost) with 'margin' (profit expressed as a percentage of selling price). The CTR (Click-Through Rate) Calculator enforces precise margin formulas: Margin = (Revenue - COGS) / Revenue, ensuring business owners do not underprice inventory or miscalculate campaign returns.

All financial modeling and campaign metrics run 100% locally on your computer or phone. Your confidential supplier wholesale costs, ad budgets, and product margin data remain strictly private, providing an executive-grade financial forecasting workbench.
