Operational Context & Core Challenge

Local trade contractors face extreme revenue leakage when job leads are missed. When a homeowner discovers a burst water pipe, failed air conditioning in mid-summer, or an electrical fault, they search Google on their phone and dial the first top-ranked contractor. If the call goes to voicemail or an online form requires 24 hours for a response, the customer immediately dials the next competitor on the list. For a company charging $1,500 to $8,000 per install or emergency repair, losing two missed calls a week represents over $150,000 in annual lost revenue.

The Engineered Solution

We build a high-speed, emergency-optimized mobile architecture that captures and qualifies inbound homeowners within 60 seconds. The system validates the caller's postal zip code against the contractor's active service territory, classifies the job as emergency vs. scheduled estimate, captures job site photos directly from the customer's phone camera, and pushes an immediate dispatch notification to on-call field technicians via SMS and webhook.

End-to-End Dispatch & Quoting Pipeline

The workflow routes emergency and quote requests through five tightly integrated automation stages:

STAGE 01

Instant Service Area Zip Code Verification

Edge Geofencing

The homeowner inputs their postal code. Client-side edge logic verifies coverage within 50ms, ensuring field technicians never waste time reviewing requests from outside their travel territory.

STAGE 02

Triage Classification (Emergency vs. Standard Quote)

Form Logic

The interface immediately separates critical active leaks/HVAC outages from non-urgent remodel consultations, routing emergency calls straight to a dedicated dispatch line.

STAGE 03

Direct Mobile Photo Upload Pipeline

Cloudflare R2 / S3

Homeowners upload equipment or damage photos directly from their mobile camera. Presigned upload URLs upload media securely with zero server load, allowing technicians to diagnose equipment before arriving.

STAGE 04

Real-Time On-Call Technician SMS Alert

Twilio Webhook

Within 8 seconds of submission, the on-call field technician receives an SMS alert containing customer address, issue summary, photo link, and a 1-tap call button.

STAGE 05

Automated Customer Acknowledgment & Dispatch Status

Transactional SMS

The customer receives an automated text message reassuring them that their request has been assigned to an on-duty technician with estimated callback timing, stopping them from calling competitors.
