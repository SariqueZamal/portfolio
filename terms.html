01 Scope of Services & Project Proposals

Digital Cron provides custom web development, high-converting landing pages, SEO-focused architectures, Website + Booking systems, website redesigns, AI voice receptionists, trained AI chatbots, AI email triage, and automated lead management pipelines for service businesses.

Every commercial engagement is defined by a written Project Proposal or Statement of Work (SOW). The SOW specifies the exact deliverables, feature milestones, turnaround timeline, and investment terms. Any subsequent feature modifications or scope additions requested outside the approved SOW will be evaluated and agreed upon via a formal written change order.

02 100% Code & Intellectual Property Ownership

We believe businesses must maintain complete control over their digital infrastructure. Upon receipt of full payment for all project milestones:

  • The Client holds 100% unrestricted intellectual property ownership of all bespoke frontend code, custom stylesheets, database configurations, workflows, and visual branding assets created specifically for the project.
  • Digital Cron will never charge recurring proprietary platform licensing fees, hosting lock-in markups, or hostage fees to release your assets.
  • Digital Cron retains ownership of pre-existing, generalized open-source utility libraries, frameworks, or proprietary boilerplate scripts used across multiple clients, granting the Client a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use and modify such utilities as integrated into their project.
Core Guarantee: You are never locked in. You receive your complete, production-ready codebase and retain full freedom to host, modify, or extend your digital systems independently.

03 Client Responsibilities & Approvals

Efficient delivery requires prompt, collaborative communication. The Client agrees to:

  • Designate a primary decision-maker authorized to review milestone deliverables and provide technical feedback.
  • Provide necessary domain access, DNS records, third-party carrier credentials, and approved copy/media assets in a timely manner.
  • Review and provide written sign-off or requested revisions within five (5) business days of receiving milestone staging previews.

04 Website Performance, Standards & Search Visibility

Digital Cron engineers websites according to clean semantic web standards, prioritizing rapid mobile page load speeds and optimal Core Web Vitals performance when hosted on modern cloud infrastructure.

While we implement rigorous on-page technical SEO foundations—including structured Schema.org JSON-LD data, semantic heading hierarchies, metadata, XML sitemaps, and Core Web Vitals optimization—the Client acknowledges that search engine indexation, crawling speed, and organic ranking positions are governed autonomously by external search engines and cannot be guaranteed by any third party.

05 Automated Communication Systems & Regulatory Compliance

When implementing automated communication systems (including AI Voice Receptionists, automated SMS notifications, and AI email triage workflows), the following operational boundaries apply:

  • Knowledge Boundaries: AI systems operate based on business knowledge provided and approved by the Client. The Client is responsible for reviewing and confirming that business hours, pricing guidelines, service procedures, and policies programmed into the assistant are accurate.
  • Non-Emergency Use: The Client explicitly agrees that AI Voice Receptionists and automated chat tools are administrative booking and inquiry triage instruments. They are not designed, certified, or intended for medical emergencies, life-safety dispatch, or legal counsel. Emergency callers must be provided direct instructions to dial local emergency services (e.g. 911, 112).
  • Telephony & Messaging Compliance: The Client agrees to comply with all applicable telecommunications, carrier, and privacy regulations (including TCPA, CAN-SPAM, CTIA guidelines, and A2P 10DLC registration requirements where applicable) regarding customer consent for automated SMS appointment notifications.

06 Invoicing, Milestone Payments & Delivery

Projects are billed on a structured milestone schedule typically divided into Kickoff Deposit, Development/Staging Review, and Final Production Deployment. Invoices are payable within seven (7) business days of receipt.

Final production deployment to the Client's public live domain, as well as the transfer of administrative credentials and source code repositories, takes place immediately following receipt of the final project milestone payment.

07 Warranties & Limitation of Liability

Digital Cron provides a 30-day post-launch technical warranty commencing upon production deployment. During this window, any defects, broken webhook links, or bugs identified in the approved scope will be resolved promptly at zero additional charge.

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Digital Cron is not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages resulting from downtime, service interruptions, or modifications caused by third-party hosting providers, domain registrars, telephony carriers, calendar software, or external API deprecations outside our reasonable engineering control.

08 Termination & Transition

Either party may terminate an engagement for cause upon fourteen (14) days' written notice if the other party breaches a material term of these Terms or the SOW. In the event of early termination, the Client is responsible for payment for all work completed up to the date of termination, and Digital Cron will deliver all completed work products and assets corresponding to paid milestones.

Any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to these terms shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the competent courts in Hyderabad, India.
